What this calculation leaves out

  • Both formulas ignore block propagation delay, orphan blocks and difficulty adjustment. They assume that the attacker and the honest side find blocks independently at the same difficulty.
  • The white paper formula assumes that the z honest blocks arrived in exactly the average time. The exact formula reflects the dispersion of that time through the negative binomial and gives a larger value for z ≥ 2 (Chapter 9, [Advanced]).
  • At q ≥ 1/2 both formulas equal 1. This is the mathematics of the "51% attack." This widget lets q go only up to 0.49.
  • There is nothing special about "6 confirmations." It is the product of two assumptions, q = 0.1 and P < 0.1%, and both are arbitrary (Rosenfeld, quoted in Chapter 9, Section 3).
