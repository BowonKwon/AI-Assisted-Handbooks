What this figure does not show

  • The avalanche is an observation that "the output looks like random numbers unrelated to the input," not a proof of preimage or collision resistance. The grounds for resistance are in Chapter 4, Section 4 (the birthday paradox) and Section 5 (the specification).
  • The mean near 128 is the same whatever the input. That a particular pair of inputs gives 101 or 150 is not a defect but the tail of the binomial distribution (of 552 flips, minimum 101, maximum 150).
  • A hash is not encryption. There is no key that takes you back to the input, and the same input always gives the same output. A short input can be matched by exhaustive search (Chapter 4, Section 2, "What a hash cannot do").
