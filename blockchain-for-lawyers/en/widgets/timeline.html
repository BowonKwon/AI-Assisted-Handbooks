What this timeline leaves out

  • Events from 2024 on. Under this book's rule, current affairs that go stale quickly are placed not in the main text but in Appendix A (trend notes).
  • Prices and market capitalization. They are on no axis (this book does not carry market prices).
  • Events whose originals could not be opened and checked were left out.
  • Year rule: for a conference paper, the conference year and the publication year can differ (Chaum 1982/1983, Merkle 1987/1988). The small print of each item gives both.
