With M = 1 (batch-1 decode), most beats go to filling and draining. In prefill, with thousands of tokens, that cost is buried and utilization climbs to nearly 90%. This counts in beats what Section 4 of the text says: "with small batches or long, thin matrices (decode!), utilization drops sharply."

What this toy simplifies

  • Only one tile is streamed. Real chips feed the next tile right behind it, overlapping fill and drain time. Even so, with a small M each weight is used only M times and then discarded, so the gain is small.
  • The time to load weights into the PEs is not counted.
  • Only one dataflow, weight-stationary, is shown. Others exist, such as output-stationary.
  • The values are small integers chosen for easy checking. The real TPU v1 does 8-bit integer multiply-accumulate.
TPU v1's 256 × 256 = 65,536 8-bit MAC array: Jouppi et al., “In-Datacenter Performance Analysis of a Tensor Processing Unit,” ISCA 2017. Follows Session 2, Section 4 of the text.
